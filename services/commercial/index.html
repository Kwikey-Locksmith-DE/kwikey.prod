Common questions

Commercial Locksmith FAQ

What's the difference between key cards and fob access?

Key cards are credit-card sized, while fobs fit on a keychain. Both can support fast credential changes when the access-control system is configured for it. Kwikey reviews door count, users, audit needs, and hardware before recommending cards, fobs, keypads, or mobile credentials.

Are your commercial lock installations ADA compliant?

Kwikey reviews the door, occupancy, hardware, and inspection notes before recommending commercial hardware. We can install ADA-aware lever and exit-hardware options, but final ADA or fire-code compliance depends on building use, local authority, and owner or inspector requirements.

Do you offer after-hours locksmith service for businesses?

Yes, when specialist availability and the job type allow it during mobile service hours. We can also schedule some business work outside customer traffic windows to reduce disruption. Call with the address, door issue, and timing constraints.

An employee left with keys. What should I do?

Rekey the doors they could access as soon as possible. Returned keys may still have copies. For multi-door businesses, we can rekey only affected areas, preserve the master system when possible, and help you deactivate electronic credentials.
