You Lost Your Mailbox Key

Mailbox keys are tiny and easy to lose. Without it, your mail piles up and anyone could be fishing through it. We open the box without damage, install a new lock, and give you fresh keys.

Why this matters more than you think

Why this matters more than you think

Your Mail Contains Your Identity

Bank statements, tax forms, medical bills, credit card offers - all sitting in an unlocked or compromised mailbox. A new lock is cheap insurance against identity theft.

Mailbox Locks Are Not Door Locks

They're smaller, use different mechanisms (tubular or wafer), and need specific tools. A regular locksmith tool won't work. We carry the right equipment for these tiny, specialized locks.

Done in One Quick Visit

Unlike waiting for your HOA or post office to send someone, we come to you and handle it in about 15 minutes. One visit, new lock, new keys, done.

Community Mailbox Rules Followed

Cluster mailboxes have a master lock for the mail carrier and individual locks for each unit. We only replace your individual lock - we never touch the carrier's master access.

Common questions

Mailbox Lock Replacement FAQ

These are the questions people ask most before booking this service. If yours isn't here, call us - we'll answer it directly.

Can you open my mailbox if I lost the key?

Yes. We use small bypass tools to open it without damaging the box. Once it's open, we replace the lock and give you new keys so you don't have this problem again.

Do I need to contact the post office first?

Usually no. If it's your personal mailbox or your apartment unit, we can replace the lock directly. The post office only manages the master lock that lets the carrier deliver - we don't touch that.

How long does it take?

About 10-15 minutes once we're on site. Mailbox locks are small and straightforward to replace. The hardest part is usually getting the old corroded one out.

Will I get multiple keys?

Yes. New mailbox locks typically come with 2 keys. If you need extras for family members or tenants, we can cut additional copies on the spot.

My mailbox lock is frozen - can you fix it or does it need replacing?

If it's seized from internal corrosion, replacement is usually the better option. A corroded lock will just freeze again. We install a weather-resistant replacement that handles the elements better.
