Common questions

Automotive Locksmith FAQ

Are aftermarket car keys as good as dealer keys?

Quality aftermarket keys can work well when the blank, chip, and programming match the vehicle. We confirm compatibility, cut and program the key when supported, then test unlock and start functions before handoff.

Why should I use a locksmith instead of the dealership?

Mobile service can often avoid towing and dealership scheduling. Compatibility depends on the vehicle and key type. We confirm the year, make, model, ownership, and programming requirements before pricing the work for Delaware and nearby Pennsylvania drivers.

Do you service hybrid and electric vehicle keys?

Hybrid and electric vehicle key support depends on the year, make, model, credential type, and programming access. Share the vehicle details and whether you have a working key, card, or fob so Kwikey can confirm whether mobile programming is supported before pricing the work.

My key turns hard in the ignition. What's wrong?

A hard-turning key can point to a worn key, worn ignition cylinder, steering-column tension, debris, or another ignition issue. Share the year, make, model, symptoms, and whether the key still turns so Kwikey can review whether mobile ignition service is a fit before pricing repair or replacement.
