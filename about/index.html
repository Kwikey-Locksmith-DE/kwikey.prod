How we work

Why we diagnose before we work

The locksmith industry has a problem. You Google "locksmith near me," call an 800-number, and someone shows up who charges three times what you expected — after they've already started drilling your lock. You feel trapped. It happens constantly.

We do it differently. When you call Kwikey, we ask what's wrong, where you are, and what kind of lock or vehicle is involved. We confirm we can help and give you an arrival window. When our specialist arrives, they look at the actual hardware, explain what's needed, and tell you the price before starting. If it doesn't work for you, you can walk away. We don't start work until you say yes.

Our shop is at 211 Maryland Ave in Wilmington. We've been here since 2009. Same location, same team doing the work. When we say "local locksmith," we mean you can drive to our shop and see the vans parked outside. That's the difference between us and a lead-gen website with a local phone number.

What we won't do

Three rules we never break

These aren't marketing slogans. They're the actual rules every job follows, every time, no exceptions. If we ever break one, call us on it.

01

You get the price before we start

Our specialist looks at the lock, door, or vehicle on-site. Then tells you exactly what it costs. You approve before we touch anything. No surprises after the fact.

02

We come to wherever you are

Home, business, parking lot, side of the road. Our vans carry the tools for your specific lock, key, or vehicle. You don't bring anything to us.

03

We test everything before leaving

Keys turn smoothly. Deadbolts throw fully. Car engines start. Smart locks connect. We don't consider a job done until you've seen it work from both sides of the door.
